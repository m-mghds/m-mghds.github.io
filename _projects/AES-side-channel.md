Security evaluation of different AES implementations against side-channel attacks.

## Evaluated Attacks

- Timing attacks
- SPA
- DPA
- CPA
- Template attacks
- Collision-based attacks

## Implementations

- T-Table AES
- BRAM AES
- Pipelined AES
- Byte-serial AES
- Software AES
