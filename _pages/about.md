I am a researcher in **hardware security and cryptographic engineering**, with an M.Sc. in Computer Architecture Engineering from the Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST).

My research focuses on the security and trustworthiness of hardware and cryptographic implementations. I am particularly interested in **physical unclonable functions (PUFs), side-channel and fault analysis, logic locking, secure RTL/FPGA design, and hardware-oriented cryptography**.

My previous research includes developing PUF architectures resilient to machine-learning modeling attacks, investigating testability-based logic locking, and designing hardware architectures for resource-constrained systems. More recently, I have been working on **ASCON implementations and side-channel evaluation**, including power-analysis and machine-learning-based attacks.

I am currently interested in pursuing PhD research at the intersection of **hardware security, cryptographic engineering, and formal methods for trustworthy hardware systems**.

### Research Interests

- Hardware Security
- Cryptographic Engineering
- Side-Channel & Fault Analysis
- Physical Unclonable Functions
- Logic Locking
- Secure FPGA / RTL Design
- Formal Methods for Hardware Security
